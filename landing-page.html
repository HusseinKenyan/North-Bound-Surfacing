Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a new driveway cost?

Every driveway is different. The price depends on the size, the surface you choose and the groundwork needed. That's why we measure up and give you a free, no-obligation quote. Book yours now and you'll also get 10% off.

Resin or tarmac, which is best for me?

Resin gives a smooth, decorative finish in a wide range of colours, and rainwater drains through it. Tarmac is hard-wearing and cost-effective, especially for larger areas. Not sure? We'll talk you through both and recommend what suits your home and budget.

Do I need planning permission?

In most cases, no. Resin bound surfaces are permeable, which is why they rarely need permission. For other surfaces we make sure water drains correctly. We'll advise you on anything that applies to your property when we quote.

Can you do the edging, patio and turf too?

Yes. We install resin, tarmac, block paving, porcelain, paved edging and artificial turf, so one team can handle the whole job.

What areas do you cover?

We cover all of Greater Manchester and Cheshire. Pop your postcode into the survey above and we'll confirm.

Is the quote really free?

Yes. Your quote is 100% free with no obligation to go ahead. And for a limited time, you'll get 10% off your installation.
