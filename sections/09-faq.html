Resin or tarmac, which is best for me?

Resin gives a smooth, decorative finish in a wide range of colours, and rainwater drains through it. Tarmac is hard-wearing and cost-effective, especially for larger areas. Not sure? We'll talk you through both and recommend what suits your home and budget.

Do I need planning permission?

In most cases, no. Resin bound surfaces are permeable, which is why they rarely need permission. For other surfaces we make sure water drains correctly. We'll advise you on anything that applies to your property when we quote.
